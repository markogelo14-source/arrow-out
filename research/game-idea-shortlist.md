# Simple iOS game ideas, from the US Top Free Games chart

Chart snapshot: Apple US Top Free Games, Oct 13 2026 (top 100), read via
[gamedropdaily.com](https://gamedropdaily.com/mobile/) and [appshunter.io](https://appshunter.io/charts/games/free).
Apple's own chart feed (rss.marketingtools.apple.com) is blocked by this cloud environment's network policy,
so these public mirrors were used instead.

## What the chart says
- Puzzle dominates: roughly 12 of the top 20 free games are simple puzzle games, mostly from small hybrid-casual studios.
- The top 6 are all one-screen puzzles: Meowdoku! (cat sudoku), Block Out! (color block sort), Magic Sort! (water sort),
  CubeAway (3D tap-away), Bus Fever Party! (traffic jam sort), Rotate Rings (twist to unlink).
- Industry data agrees: sort, block and screw puzzles are the fastest growing puzzle subgenres in 2026, while match-3 is
  saturated ([Mobidictum, Jul 2026](https://mobidictum.com/sort-block-screw-mechanics-puzzle-games-market/),
  [Cinevva trends 2026](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/casual-games-trends-2026)). Block Blast! was the top ad earner
  (~$127M Jan to May 2026) with an ad-only model, so a simple puzzle can make money without in-app purchases.

## Shortlist (easiest first)

### 1. Arrow escape (recommended)
- Charting now: Amaze GO! (#13), Arrows – Puzzle Escape (#27).
- How it plays: a grid of arrows; tap one and it slides off the board in its direction, unless something blocks it. Clear the board.
- Why it fits a first app: pure grid logic, no physics, flat 2D art. Levels can be generated by code (build a solvable
  board by placing arrows in reverse removal order), so no hand-made level design is needed.
- Build: SwiftUI alone is enough. Roughly a weekend for a playable prototype.
- Twist ideas: themed pieces (fish, cars, ants), a daily challenge, move limits.

### 2. Block puzzle (8x8, drag pieces, clear rows and columns)
- Charting now: Block Blast! (#9 to #11), Woodoku Blast (#72), Sand Blocks (#29).
- Why: endless mode means zero level design, and it is the proven ad-revenue champion.
- Risk: very crowded; needs a twist (a theme, sand physics, or a daily puzzle) to stand out.
- Build: SwiftUI or SpriteKit for drag and drop. Simple.

### 3. Color / water sort
- Charting now: Magic Sort! (#3), Fish Sort Puzzle (#25), Goods Puzzle: Sort Challenge (#99).
- How it plays: pour colors between tubes until each tube holds one color.
- Why: the biggest growing puzzle subgenre; rules fit in a few dozen lines; levels can be generated by shuffling a solved state.
- Risk: very crowded, so the theme is the whole differentiator.
- Build: SwiftUI with simple animations.

### 4. Themed logic puzzle (sudoku variant)
- Charting now: Meowdoku! (#1), Sudoku.com (#55).
- Why: the current #1 is sudoku with cats. A familiar logic rule set plus a charming theme beats a new mechanic.
- Build: SwiftUI; needs a puzzle generator and solver (well documented algorithms).

### 5. Ring rotate / unlink
- Charting now: Rotate Rings (#6).
- Why: fresh mechanic at the top of the chart, less cloned so far.
- Risk: geometry and level design are harder; a good second game rather than a first.
- Build: SpriteKit.

### Skipped
Bus/traffic jam sort, merge games, 3D tap-away (CubeAway), and anything with real-money or casino mechanics: more art,
3D or content than a first app should take on, or extra App Store review rules.
