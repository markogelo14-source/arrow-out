Support

Stuck on a board, found a bug, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you. Email support@example.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can, usually within two working days.

It helps if you include your device model, iOS version and the level number if your message is about a specific board.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap an arrow and it slides off the board in the direction it points. If another arrow is in the way, it bumps and stays where it is. Clear every arrow to finish the board. Our how to play guide has tips.

Is every level actually solvable?

Yes. Every board is built backwards from an empty grid, so a solution always exists. Here's how that works.

How do hearts and hints work?

You have 3 hearts per board. Tapping an arrow whose path is blocked costs a heart. You also get 3 hints per board: a hint highlights an arrow that can escape right now.

What is the Daily Puzzle?

One bigger board every day, the same for every player.

I'm stuck. What should I do?

Look for arrows with a clear path to the edge and remove those first, then see which arrows they were blocking. If you still can't see it, restart the board: a fresh look often helps.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Arrowly works completely offline.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Progress is saved on your device. If you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, your progress comes with it.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See our privacy policy for details.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases on the App Store are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
