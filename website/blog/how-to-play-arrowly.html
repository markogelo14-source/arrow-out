How to play Arrowly: the rules and 6 tips for any board

Arrowly has one rule, and you can learn it in five seconds: tap an arrow and it slides off the board in the direction it points, unless another arrow is in the way. Clear every arrow and the board is solved.

The rule is simple. The boards are not always. Here is how to get better at them.

The rules, in full

  • Every arrow points up, down, left or right.
  • When you tap an arrow, it travels in a straight line all the way off the edge of the board.
  • Arrows can be 1 to 3 cells long, so a long arrow can block several lanes at once.
  • If any arrow sits anywhere on that line, the tapped arrow bumps into it, bounces back, and you lose a heart. You have 3 hearts per board.
  • Stuck? You get 3 hints per board. A hint highlights an arrow that can escape right now.
  • The board is cleared when every arrow has left.

Six tips for any board

1. Start with the free arrows

Before you tap anything, scan for arrows with nothing between them and the edge. These are always safe to remove. On most boards, a few arrows on the outer ring point outward and can leave immediately.

2. Ask "who is blocking whom?"

For any stuck arrow, follow its line to the edge and note which arrows are in the way. Those blockers have to go first. Thinking in terms of blockers turns a crowded board into a short to-do list.

3. Follow the chain

Every time you remove an arrow, look at what it was blocking. Often the arrow you just cleared was the only thing holding up another one, which was holding up another. Following these chains is how big boards fall apart in a few satisfying taps.

4. Work from the outside in

Arrows near the edges have shorter paths and fewer potential blockers. Clearing the outer ring first usually opens up the middle.

5. Think before you tap

A bump costs a heart, so trace the path with your eyes first. If you're down to your last heart and still unsure, spend a hint instead of guessing.

6. Take a step back

If you've been staring at the same corner for a while, look at the whole board instead. The arrow you need is often on the other side, quietly blocking the one you're stuck on.

Can a board ever be impossible?

No. Every board in Arrowly is built so that a solution exists, and you can never get yourself stuck by removing arrows in the "wrong" order: taking an arrow away only ever clears paths, never blocks them. Curious how that works? We explain it in How we make sure every level can be solved.
