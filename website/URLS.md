# Website URLs for App Store Connect

Hosting: GitHub Pages from the `markogelo14-source/arrow-out` repo. The workflow in `.github/workflows/pages.yml` publishes this `website/` folder on every push to `main`.
Pages must be switched on once: repo Settings > Pages > Source: **GitHub Actions**.

| Purpose | URL | App Store Connect field |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | https://markogelo14-source.github.io/arrow-out/ | Marketing URL (optional) |
| Privacy policy | https://markogelo14-source.github.io/arrow-out/privacy.html | App Privacy > Privacy Policy URL |
| Support | https://markogelo14-source.github.io/arrow-out/support.html | Support URL |

The privacy policy matches v1.0: no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases; progress is stored on the device only.
The contact email is still the placeholder `support@example.com` until Marko gives a real one.
