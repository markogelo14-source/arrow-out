How to play

The rules fit on a sticky note. The puzzles take a little longer.

  1. Tap any arrow. It slides straight off the board in the direction it points.
  2. Mind the traffic. If another arrow is in its path, it bounces back and you lose a heart. You have three per board.
  3. Clear the board. Work out the right order and send every arrow home.
