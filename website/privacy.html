Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the Arrowly app for iPhone and iPad ("the app") and this website collect, and how it is used. The short version: we don't collect any personal information.

Information we collect

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Your game progress (for example which levels you have completed and your settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. If you use iCloud, Apple may back up this data as part of your device backup under Apple's privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not contain third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising code, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics about app downloads, usage and crashes if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device settings. This information does not identify you.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. Like any website, the server that hosts it may keep standard access logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any other age.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add optional features that need data), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@example.com.
