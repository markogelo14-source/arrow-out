Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Arrowly mobile game ("the app") and the Arrowly website at markogelo14-source.github.io/arrow-out ("the website") handle information about you. We wrote it to be short and readable. The summary:

  • The app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to our servers.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

In this policy "we", "us" and "our" refer to that developer.

2. Information the app handles

Data we do not collect

The app does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or any device identifier for advertising (IDFA). It does not ask for any permissions.

Data stored only on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following on your device using Apple's standard storage (UserDefaults):

  • the levels you have completed and your current level;
  • which Daily Puzzles you have solved, and your streak;
  • your settings, such as whether haptic feedback is on.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us, so we cannot see it. It may be included in your device backup (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under the Apple Privacy Policy. To delete it, delete the app. All of it is removed immediately.

Information Apple may share with us

Apple provides developers with download numbers, sales reports and, only if you have chosen to share them in your device's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. This information is aggregated or anonymous and does not identify you. Apple's handling of it is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. We use it only to fix bugs and improve the app.

3. Information the website handles

  • Hosting. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page and protect the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs.
  • Fonts. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. The website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising tools, and stores nothing in your browser.

4. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you. We keep the conversation for up to 12 months after it ends (in case you follow up) and then delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Our email is provided by [EMAIL PROVIDER, e.g. Google (Gmail)], which processes messages on our behalf.

5. Legal basis (EU and UK users)

  • Saving your progress on your device: necessary to provide the game you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). It never reaches us.
  • Answering your emails: our legitimate interest in providing support (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Website delivery and security (hosting logs, fonts): our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f)).

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, and we do not use it for advertising. The only third parties involved are the service providers named above (Apple, GitHub, Google and our email provider), each for the purpose described.

7. International transfers

GitHub, Google and Apple may process data in the United States and other countries. These companies rely on recognised safeguards for transfers out of the EU, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because the app keeps everything on your device, you can delete your game data at any time by deleting the app. For anything else (for example deleting our emails with you), contact us at the address above. We will respond within one month.

If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Croatia this is the Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information, and we collect no personal information through the app. You have the right to know, delete and correct personal information and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

9. Children

Arrowly is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. We keep emails in a password-protected account with two-factor authentication.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app or website handles data (for example by adding a feature that needs data), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before that change goes live, and update the App Store privacy details to match.
