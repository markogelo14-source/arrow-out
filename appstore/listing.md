## Description (max 4000)
Tap an arrow and it slides off the board in the direction it points. Simple. Unless another arrow is in the way.

Arrowly is a calm, one-thumb logic puzzle about finding the right order. Every board is a tangle of arrows. Spot the one with a clear path, tap it away, then find the next, until the board is empty. One wrong tap costs a heart, so think before you tap.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap any arrow to send it sliding in its direction.
• If another arrow blocks the way, it bounces back and you lose a heart.
• Clear every arrow before your 3 hearts run out.
• Stuck? Use a hint to highlight an arrow that can escape right now.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Easy to learn in seconds, satisfying to master.
• Endless levels, every one guaranteed solvable.
• Boards grow from tiny 4x4 warm-ups to big 8x10 brain-burners.
• Long arrows block several lanes at once, so every move matters.
• A new Daily Puzzle every day, the same board for every player.
• Satisfying haptic feedback on every move.
• No timers. Play at your own pace, offline, anywhere.
• No ads, no account, no data collected.

Perfect for fans of logic puzzles, sliding puzzles and tap-away games who want a quick, relaxing challenge.
